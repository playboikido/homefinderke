Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 26, 2026

Welcome to HomeFinder KE. We value your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our website and services ("the Platform"), and it is written to align with Kenya's Data Protection Act, 2019 (DPA), enforced by the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner (ODPC).

1. Information We Collect

Information you provide directly: your name, email address, phone number, and profile details (bio, profile picture, county/town, language and display preferences) when you register an account; listing details (location, price, photos, description) if you list a property or business service; and messages you send to other users or to our support/AI assistant. Business accounts must additionally provide proof of ownership or legitimate operation of the business being listed.

Information collected automatically: your IP address and browser/device information; a device fingerprint (a hashed combination of IP address and browser identifier), used to recognize returning devices and protect your account from unauthorized logins; cookies and session data (see Section 3); and general usage data to help us improve the Platform.

We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to operate, maintain, and improve our services — including to facilitate communications between users, verify listings and Business accounts, recognize known devices and detect suspicious logins for account security, provide location-based features (such as nearby moving services), and send notifications related to your account. We do not sell your personal data to third parties.

3. Cookies and Tracking

We use cookies and similar tracking technologies to preserve your session, remember your preferences (such as theme and language), and support security features. You can control cookie preferences in your browser settings; disabling them may limit some Platform functionality, such as staying logged in.

4. Third-Party Services

We work with third-party providers who process limited data on our behalf, including Cloudinary (image and media hosting), Resend (transactional emails), Google reCAPTCHA (spam and bot prevention), Neon (database hosting), Render (application hosting), and M-Pesa or other payment providers (for voluntary support payments or sponsored listing fees). These services handle your data in accordance with their own privacy policies. Some providers may process or store data outside Kenya; where this occurs, we take reasonable steps to ensure an adequate level of data protection, consistent with the DPA's cross-border transfer requirements.

5. Data Retention

We retain your account data for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, we remove personally identifiable data within a reasonable period, except where retention is necessary for fraud prevention and dispute resolution, legal or regulatory compliance, or legitimate business records such as archived listing content described in our Terms of Service.

6. Your Rights

Under the Data Protection Act, 2019, you have the right to access the personal data we hold about you; request correction of inaccurate data; request deletion of your data, subject to Section 5 above; object to or restrict certain processing; request a copy of your data in a portable format; and lodge a complaint with the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner (ODPC) if you believe your data has been mishandled. To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the details in Section 8.

7. Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your personal data from unauthorized access, loss, or misuse, including device recognition and rate-limited login attempts. However, no internet transmission or storage method is 100% secure.

8. Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns regarding this Privacy Policy, or wish to exercise any of your rights under Section 6, you can reach out to us at homefinder.ke.help@gmail.com.
