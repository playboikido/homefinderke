About Us

About HomeFinder KE

HomeFinder KE is a community-driven platform that helps Kenyans discover apartments and rental houses across Kenya.

Find a Home. Help Someone Find Theirs.

The platform allows communities to contribute apartment information to help others avoid broker fees and find houses more easily.

Our Story & Inspiration

The Problem We Saw

Finding a house in Kenya — especially in Nairobi — meant endless phone calls, broker fees, and wasted trips. People were spending weeks just to view a single listing, often arriving to find it already taken or misrepresented.

The Community Solution

HomeFinder KE was born from the belief that Kenyans can help each other. Property owners list their spaces directly — no middlemen, no inflated broker fees. Tenants browse real listings with real photos and real contacts.

What We Cover

From bedsitters in Eastlands to apartments in Westlands, from Mombasa's coastline to Kisumu's lakeside — our listings span all 47 counties so every Kenyan can find a home that fits their life and budget.

Our Commitment

Every listing on HomeFinder KE goes through a review process before it goes live. We verify that listings are genuine, prices are accurate, and contact details work — so you never waste a trip.

How HomeFinder KE Works

1. Choose How You'll Use It

When you sign up, you pick one of two account types: Resident if you're searching for a home (and can list your own property too), or Business if you offer a home-related service like moving, furniture, cleaning, or household goods.

2. Sign Up or Log In

New here? Tap "Get Started" to pick your account type, agree to our terms, and create an account in under a minute — with email or Google. Already registered? Just log in from the same menu to pick up where you left off.

3. Residents: Search or List

Browse listings for free, message property owners directly, and save favorites. Have a spare room or plot? List it yourself — no approval needed to publish, though listings are reviewed afterward for accuracy.

4. Businesses: Get Discovered

Moving companies, furniture vendors, and other home-service businesses list in our Moving Essentials directory, where residents settling into a new home find you — after a quick verification of your business.
