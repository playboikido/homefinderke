Common Issues

I can't log in / forgot my password

Use "Forgot Password" on the login screen to reset it by email. If you signed up with Google, use the "Continue with Google" button instead of a password. Still stuck? Chat with the Help Assistant or file a report below.

My listing hasn't gone live yet

New listings are reviewed by our team before they appear publicly — this usually takes a short while, longer during busy periods. Check that your photos passed the AI quality check when you submitted. If it's been more than 48 hours, file a report and we'll look into it.

My account was suspended or restricted

Accounts are usually restricted after a report or a policy violation. You'll see a message explaining why when you try to act on the site. If you believe it's a mistake, file a report under "Account Locked or Suspended" and our team will review it.

My verification (ID or business document) was rejected

Make sure the photo is clear, well-lit, and shows the full document with no cropping or glare. Re-upload from Settings → Verification. If it keeps failing, file a report under "ID or Business Verification Issue."

I'm not getting notifications

Check Settings → Notifications to confirm they're enabled, and check your email spam folder for messages from us. If everything looks right but nothing arrives, file a report under "Notifications Not Working."

Someone is scamming me or acting suspiciously

Never send money outside the app, share your M-Pesa PIN, or send your ID to someone you haven't verified in person. Stop contact immediately and file an "Suspected Scam or Fraud" report — include usernames and screenshots if you have them.
